# Kovarti PM — Competitive Analysis

**Last updated:** August 29, 2026

---

## OnePlan (oneplan.ai)

**Tagline:** "Plan. Fund. Execute." — One Platform for Strategy, Resources, and Execution.

**Target market:** Enterprise PMOs, IT departments, C-suite executives, large organizations.

**AI assistant:** Sofia AI — portfolio rebalancing, forecasting, risk surfacing.

**Integrations:** Microsoft Teams, Planner, Project, Azure DevOps, Jira, Smartsheet, monday.com, Asana, Wrike, Trello.

| | **Kovarti PM** | **OnePlan** |
|---|---|---|
| Free trial | 14 days, self-serve | Request a demo |

### Where Kovarti Wins

- **Price for individuals and small teams** — No 3-user minimum. $29/mo gets all features vs $30/user/mo.
- **AI depth at lower tiers** — Monte Carlo, EVM What-If, AI risk scanning included for all paid users. OnePlan gates these behind Enterprise.
- **No enterprise gatekeeping** — What-If scenarios, audit logs, and advanced features available to all paid users.
- **Simpler onboarding** — Self-serve signup with free trial. No "request a demo" wall.
- **Consultant-friendly** — Designed for individual PMs and small firms, not just large organizations.

### Where OnePlan Wins

- **Enterprise integrations** — Deep Microsoft ecosystem (Teams, Planner, Project, Azure DevOps).
- **Portfolio-level planning** — Stronger at multi-project portfolio governance for large organizations.
- **Brand recognition** — Established player in the enterprise PPM market.
- **Financial governance** — More mature budgeting, funding, and financial workflows.
- **Scale** — Better suited for organizations managing 50+ projects across departments.

### Positioning Strategy

Kovarti PM is not competing head-to-head with OnePlan. They sell top-down to enterprises. Kovarti sells to the customers they ignore:

- Individual consultants who need professional PM tools
- Small firms (1-10 people) who can't justify $30/user/month and a sales call
- PMs who want enterprise-grade features (Monte Carlo, EVM, AI) at freelancer-friendly pricing

**Key message:** "Enterprise PM features at freelancer-friendly pricing."
