What Kovarti Does

Kovarti is an AI project management platform that combines enterprise-grade scheduling with intelligent automation. Whether you run waterfall, agile, or hybrid projects, Kovarti gives you the tools to plan, track, and deliver with confidence — powered by an AI engine that learns from your data and gets smarter over time.

At the core is AI scheduling — describe your project scope and Kovarti's AI generates a complete work breakdown structure with tasks, durations, and predecessor links in seconds. Need to adjust? Drag tasks on the interactive Gantt chart, and the engine recalculates the critical path and flags conflicts automatically. This is predictive scheduling that goes beyond static timelines: Monte Carlo simulations run thousands of scenarios to forecast your real completion date at P50, P80, and P95 confidence levels, giving you honest project timeline prediction instead of wishful thinking.

Automatic task generation saves hours of manual planning. Upload a meeting transcript and Kovarti extracts action items, decisions, and risks — then turns them into tracked tasks with assignees and due dates. Import an Excel file or MS Project export and the smart column mapper recognizes your fields instantly. Break down an epic into subtasks with one click using the AI task breakdown tool.

Dependency mapping is built into every view. Define finish-to-start, start-to-start, finish-to-finish, or start-to-finish relationships with lag days, and see them rendered as arrows on the Gantt chart, network diagram, and critical path analysis. The engine validates every link — no circular dependencies, no orphaned predecessors — so your schedule stays logically sound as it evolves.

Meet Mjuzi, your AI PM assistant. Mjuzi lives in a chat panel on every page and understands your project context. Ask it to create tasks, scan for risks, generate status reports, or explain what's driving your schedule variance — and it takes action using real project data, not generic templates. Mjuzi remembers your preferences, learns from corrections, and integrates findings from autonomous risk and resource scanners that run in the background.
